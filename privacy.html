Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Lab Try AI is run by an individual based in Ontario, Canada. This page explains what data the site handles.

The prompt builder

Everything you type into the builder stays in your browser. It isn't sent to us. The site saves your last inputs and your Pro unlock status in your browser's local storage so they're there next time. You can clear them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

AI chatbots

If you click "Open in ChatGPT/Claude/Grok/Gemini", your prompt goes to that third-party service under its own privacy policy. We don't receive it.

Purchases and licence keys

Payments are handled by Gumroad. We don't see or store your card details. When you enter a licence key, your browser sends it directly to Gumroad's licence API to check that it's valid. Gumroad shares your purchase details (such as email and order info) with us as the seller.

Email signup

If you join the email list, we use your email only to send product updates and tips, and you can unsubscribe at any time. At the time of writing, the signup form isn't connected to an email provider yet. If and when one is connected, we'll name the provider here.

Analytics

At the time of writing, the site uses no analytics or tracking cookies. If we add privacy-friendly analytics (for example Plausible or Cloudflare Web Analytics), we'll list it here.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA), you can ask what personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. Contact our contact email.
