FAQ

Do I need to pay for ChatGPT or another AI?

No. The prompts work with free tiers of ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Gemini. Paid tiers usually give longer, better answers.

Does this tool run the AI for me?

No — it builds the prompt. You paste it into your AI of choice. That keeps it fast, private and cheap.

Are the platform limits accurate?

Prompts follow Etsy's 140-character titles and 13 tags of up to 20 characters, and KDP's 200-character title+subtitle, 7 keyword slots (50 characters each) and 4,000-character description limit. Always double-check AI output before publishing — AI can miscount.

Can I use AI-written content on Etsy and KDP?

Both allow it with conditions: KDP asks you to disclose AI-generated content when publishing, and Etsy requires you to be transparent about how items are made. Check each platform's current policy.
